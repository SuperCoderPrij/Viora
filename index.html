The idea behind Viora

Numbers are easy.
Meaning takes care.

We encounter more health and activity information than ever, yet a number on its own can be hard to make sense of. Viora is a project exploring how accessible hardware and clear explanations might help people learn about everyday wellness.

We’re starting with two small DIY devices, pairing hands-on experimentation with a simple, human-centered way to share what we learn.

⌕

Understand

Clarity over complexity

Present everyday wellness ideas in plain language, with useful context instead of intimidating jargon.

⌁

Experiment

Learn by making

Use approachable components to explore how sensing, software, and careful design can work together.

◎

Respect

People first, always

Treat readings as one part of a person’s story, and communicate the limits of a prototype clearly.

Why this work matters

Health tech can feel
more human.

Viora aims to make wellness technology feel less distant: easier to explore, easier to learn from, and more honest about what a sensor can tell us.

✓Make learning hands-onShow how everyday sensing can be built and explored.
✓Make information approachableExplain concepts without making people decode a dashboard.
✓Encourage thoughtful usePair curiosity with context, privacy, and clear limitations.
